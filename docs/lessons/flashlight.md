## What You'll Need

- Arduino
- Breadboard
- LED
- resistor
- jumper wires

## LED Tip

LEDs only work in one direction.

Long leg  = Positive (+)
Short leg = Negative (-)

![led](../assets/components/led.jpg)


## Breadboard Tip

A breadboard lets us connect parts without gluing them together.
