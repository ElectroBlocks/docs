# Mini Golf

## What you will learn

- How use an IR motion sensoe
- How to make an Arduino respond when something happens
- If Blocks
- How to use a servo

## Bill of Materials

![kit](../assets/mini-golf/kit.png)

- 1 x [Arduino with cable](../what-is-an-arduino/)
- 1 x Servo
- 1 x IR Sensor
- 1 x Small Box
- 1 x Pingpong ball
- 6 x wires

## Build Steps

1\. Connect a jumper wire from the Orange wire of the servo to pin 7 of the Arduino.

![step 1](../assets/mini-golf/step1.png)

2\. Connect a jumper wire from the Red wire of the servo to pin 5v of the Arduino.

![step 2](../assets/mini-golf/step2.png)

3\. Connect a jumper wire from the brown wire of the servo to one of the GND pins on the Arduino.

![step 3](../assets/mini-golf/step3.png)

4\. Connect a jumper wire from the out pin of the ir motion sensor to pin 8 of the Arduino.

![step 4](../assets/mini-golf/step4.png)

5\. Connect a jumper wire from the GND pin of the ir motion sensor to pin GND of the Arduino.

![step 5](../assets/mini-golf/step5.png)


6\. Connect a jumper wire from the VCC pin of the ir motion sensor to pin 3.3v of the Arduino.

![step 6](../assets/mini-golf/step6.png)

7\. Write the code and make sure the servo moves when you put something in front of it.


<video controls >
<source src="https://storage.googleapis.com/noah-education-videos/electroblocks/mini-golf/step-7-coding.mp4">

8\. Take your box and lay it open on the table.

![step 8](../assets/mini-golf/step8-box.png)

9\. Tape up the sides of the box.

![step 9](../assets/mini-golf/step9-tape-sides.png)

10\. Cut hole into box so that the pingpong ball can fit into it.

![step 10](../assets/mini-golf/step10-cut-whole-in-box.png)


11\. Cut a flap in the box for the servo.

![step 10](../assets/mini-golf/step-10-cut-flap.png)

12\. Tape the servo motor to the flap and use way more tape than what is in the picture.

![step 11](../assets/mini-golf/step11-attach-servo-use-more-tape.png)

13\. Test the servo motor and ensure that it works.

14\. Attach the sensor to the box so that it is pointing up at the whole.

![step 13](../assets/mini-golf/step13-attach-sensor.png)


15\. Test out the step and make sure it works with the ball.

16\. Create the flag

![step 16](../assets/mini-golf/step16-create-flag.png)

17\. Attach the flag to the wooden dowel.

![step 17](../assets/mini-golf/step17-attach-flag.png)

![step 18](../assets/mini-golf/step18-attach-servo-horn.png)

18\. Tape the sides and bottom ramp of the box.
